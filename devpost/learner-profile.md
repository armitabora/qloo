# Learner Profile

## Initial Idea
VibeGuide — an AI travel concierge powered by the Qloo API. The traveler enters a destination city (e.g., Tokyo, London, New York) and 2–3 cultural anchors they love (favorite artists, films, aesthetics). The app queries Qloo to map those affinities across domains and surfaces matching local spots — cafes, record shops, indie restaurants, art venues. MVP is a clean single-page app: input taste profile, hit generate, receive a curated 1-day itinerary with a brief explanation of why each venue matches their vibe. Scoped as an afternoon proof-of-concept.
