External Intelligence & Presentation APIs

Qloo Taste Intelligence API

Provides raw cross-domain affinity mapping. Converts cultural seeds (music, film, vibe tags) into high-affinity physical venues located in Tokyo and New York.

Google Gemini API

Presentation & storytelling layer. Takes user anchors + Qloo venue metadata to synthesize the "Why It Fits Your Vibe" narratives for Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night.

The Hackathon Demo Safety Net

🛡️ Built-in Offline Fallback Dataset

To eliminate any chance of live demo recording failure due to external API timeouts, rate-limits, or WiFi drops, FastAPI is equipped with pre-computed authentic itineraries for Tokyo and New York in fallback_data.py. If an upstream service stalls, the app automatically serves rich, verified data instantly.
